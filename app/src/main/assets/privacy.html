Privacy Policy — DevxyzDNS

Last updated: October 17, 2026

DevxyzDNS collects nothing.

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no trackers.
  • Your settings (selected DNS provider, custom servers, app exclusions, toggles) are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They never leave your phone.
  • DNS queries are sent to the provider you select — that is the entire job of a DNS changer. The app itself does not log, store, or transmit your queries anywhere else.
  • The optional Network Info screen fetches your public IP from api.ipify.org to display it; nothing is stored.

If you have questions, contact us via the in-app Send Feedback option.
